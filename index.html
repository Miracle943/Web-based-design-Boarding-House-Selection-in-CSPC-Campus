FAQ

The group of researchers behind the website works closely with the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Nabua and SASO from CSPC to ensure that all boarding houses listed on the website are registered and accredited. The LGU conducts regular inspections to ensure that the boarding houses meet safety and health standards.

The concern is that boarding houses that are not registered with the LGU Nabua may face problems like inadequate amenities, a lack of safety regulations, and lacking the providing of student safety regulations inside the boarding house. Not to mention that SASO relies heavily on the LGU Nabua for the list of boarding houses that are registered.

To register your boarding house, you need to visit the Local Government Unit (LGU) office in Nabua and inquire about the registration process. The criteria for registration may include having a valid business permit, complying with safety and health regulations, and providing necessary documentation such as proof of ownership or lease agreement.

SASO helps students find safe and legal boarding houses by maintaining a list of accredited boarding houses, conducting inspections, and providing information to students of CSPC.
